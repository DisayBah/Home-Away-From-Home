BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Student viewing checklist

  • Confirm the exact address and distance to campus.
  • Ask for the total cost, deposit and payment schedule.
  • Check water, electricity, sanitation and internet arrangements.
  • Ask about security, visitors, noise and house rules.
  • Read any agreement carefully before paying.
