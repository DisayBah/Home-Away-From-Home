OUR PURPOSE

Helping students feel more prepared.

Home Away From Home is a student accommodation website concept designed to make accommodation considerations easier to understand.

WHO WE ARE

A guide for a big student decision.

Moving away from home or finding a place near campus can be a major step. Students need clear information about costs, location, safety and the conditions of a property before deciding where to live.

This concept website presents accommodation ideas and practical questions students can use when comparing possible places to stay.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What this website aims to encourage

Clarity

Use clear headings, readable text and straightforward explanations.

Informed choices

Encourage students to check important details instead of relying on photographs alone.

Student wellbeing

Highlight the importance of safety, comfort, study needs and affordability.

Project note: This is an academic demonstration website. Sample listings and descriptions are not verified accommodation offers.
