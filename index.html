MAKE AN INFORMED CHOICE

Choose a space that supports your goals.

Before choosing accommodation, compare the distance to campus, transport options, total cost, security, water and electricity supply, internet access and the rules of the property.

  • Think about your monthly budget.
  • Visit the property if possible.
  • Ask about bills, deposits and house rules.
  • Confirm the location and safety arrangements.
